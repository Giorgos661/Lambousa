Discover Lambousa


Embark on a journey into a mesmerizing adventure with Lambousa, an old vessel that sailed the waters of Limassol and left an irremovable mark in the history of the port city.

Our website is a reconstruction of time, a walk through centuries of history and legacy that the Lambousa bears. This is not just a ship; it is a lifeline that symbolizes a poignant reflection of radical changes in lifestyle and work that ages throughout history but challenges time. A ship is an immortal monument to the fishing art that merges with culture.

Today, the ship is under the patronage of the Limassol municipality but holds so much more value that symbolizes hardship, survival, and time. Its old planks that creak when walking over them tell much more than can be put into words. It tells stories of a profession that has normalized challenge and hope. It is a work of art that celebrates history and negotiation with life and death over centuries.

Lambousa is an art piece with much more ideology than its wooden frame and barrels of rust. Our aim is more than documentation of the ship; we strive to keep the education and understanding of the art of fishing afloat.
