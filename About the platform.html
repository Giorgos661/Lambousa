Discover Lambousa


Welcome to our innovative online world centered on the timeless vessel known as Lambousa, the traditional fishing boat that has now become an integral part of Cyprus's maritime history. This project is designed to provide a detailed digital exploration of Lambousa, which will enable us to see how it has influenced the world and how the maritime architecture of the island is unique, and how the tradition of fishing has become a part of the cultural heritage that has continued to exist.

Lambousa is not simply a fishing trawler; it is the emblem of perseverance and the historical continuity, now in the hands of the Limassol Municipality. Highly acknowledged for its importance, our platform uses the latest digital technologies to document every detail of Lambousa, from the old wooden planks to the stories in the rusted anchors, thus, gives a way back to an age where the sea was a way of life.

By launching this initiative, we will be able to increase the community’s involvement with our maritime history, at the same time, we will be able to teach digital literacy to all age groups, but especially the youth. Through the participation of the local community in the preservation of the heritage and the digital skills training of the people, we make the residents feel more attached to their heritage.

We are not just saving the physical appearance, but our approach goes beyond that to preserve other things too. Here we get into the story of Lambousa and its past voyages, the fishermen's traditions and how all of these have contributed to the formation of our identity. By means of great narratives and interactive encounters, we seek to have a colorful and informative life-changing experience for visitors from the four corners of the world.

We ask you to join us and take a journey through the digital seaplanes of Lambousa. You will get to know the town's past, understand the art of fishing, and see the connection between our community and the sea. This project is not just a digital archive, it is a commemoration of maritime culture, a protection of the heritage, and a search for the future possibilities. Come and have a time with us, where we are celebrating the past, the present and, the future of Lambousa, the place where history and technology are combined.
