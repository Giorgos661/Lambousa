Lambousa Heritage Project


The Lambousa Heritage Project is an initiative dedicated to preserving and showcasing the history of the Lambousa, a significant fishing trawler in Limassol's maritime history. This project, overseen by the Limassol municipality, focuses on the factual and historical aspects of the vessel, highlighting its role and impact in the local fishing industry and community.

Lambousa, a long-serving trawler in the Mediterranean, has played a crucial part in the fishing practices and economic life of Limassol. The project aims to document and educate about the trawler's design, construction, and the various fishing techniques employed over the years. It also explores the changes in maritime technology and how Lambousa adapted to these over time.

Through this initiative, the Lambousa Heritage Project seeks to connect the younger generation with their maritime heritage, emphasizing the importance of preserving such historical vessels. The project serves as a resource for understanding the evolution of fishing in Cyprus and the Mediterranean, offering insights into the socio-economic aspects of the industry.

The Lambousa Heritage Project is a blend of historical preservation and educational outreach, ensuring that the legacy of this iconic trawler continues to inform and inspire future generations about Limassol's rich maritime past.
